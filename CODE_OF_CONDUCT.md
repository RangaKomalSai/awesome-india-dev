# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone contributing to this
list, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues and pull-request reviews.
- Assume good faith; disagree about entries without personal attacks.
- Keep the list neutral — this is a shared reference, not an ad space.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Spamming the list with self-promotional or low-quality entries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or reject contributions that don't follow this
code, and may block repeat offenders. To report an issue, open a private
report to the maintainers via the repository's contact options.
