# Awesome India Dev [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of APIs, tools, and services for developers building software for the Indian market.

Building for India means integrating a specific stack: UPI and homegrown payment rails, Aadhaar/PAN-based KYC, the Account Aggregator framework, government data APIs, ONDC, and more. This list gathers those building blocks in one neutral, community-maintained place — so you can find the right service without wading through vendor blog posts.

Descriptions are vendor-neutral and factual. Inclusion is not an endorsement. Verify regulatory licensing (RBI, UIDAI, etc.) and pricing yourself before integrating.

## Payments

- [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com) - Payment gateway with UPI, cards, netbanking, subscriptions, and payouts.
- [Cashfree Payments](https://www.cashfree.com) - Payment gateway and payouts platform with UPI and settlement APIs.
- [PhonePe Payment Gateway](https://www.phonepe.com/business/payment-gateway/) - Payment gateway from the PhonePe UPI ecosystem.
- [Juspay](https://juspay.io) - Payment orchestration and checkout infrastructure used by large merchants.
- [PayU India](https://payu.in) - Payment gateway supporting UPI, cards, EMI, and BNPL.
- [Instamojo](https://www.instamojo.com) - Payments and storefronts aimed at small businesses and creators.
- [Setu](https://setu.co) - UPI deep links, collections, and embedded finance building blocks.
- [NPCI](https://www.npci.org.in) - The umbrella organisation behind UPI, IMPS, BBPS, RuPay, and other retail rails.

## Identity, KYC & Verification

- [Sandbox](https://sandbox.co.in) - Unified KYC/KYB APIs across Aadhaar, PAN, MCA, DigiLocker, and bank verification.
- [Signzy](https://signzy.com) - Digital onboarding, video KYC, OCR, and fraud-detection APIs.
- [IDfy](https://www.idfy.com) - Identity verification, video KYC, and background-verification APIs.
- [HyperVerge](https://hyperverge.co) - Face-match, liveness, and document-verification APIs.
- [Gridlines](https://gridlines.io) - KYC/KYB verification APIs for PAN, Aadhaar, GST, and bank accounts.
- [AuthBridge](https://www.authbridge.com) - KYC and employee/background screening APIs.

## Account Aggregator & Financial Data

- [Setu Account Aggregator](https://setu.co/data/account-aggregator) - Consent-based financial data access under the AA framework.
- [Finvu](https://finvu.in) - RBI-licensed Account Aggregator.
- [Perfios](https://www.perfios.com) - Bank-statement analysis and financial data intelligence.
- [Sahamati](https://sahamati.org.in) - Industry alliance for the Account Aggregator ecosystem (specs and participant directory).

## Government & Public Data APIs

- [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in) - Government-issued document storage and verification.
- [Open Government Data (data.gov.in)](https://data.gov.in) - Datasets and APIs published by Indian government bodies.
- [UIDAI](https://uidai.gov.in) - Aadhaar authority; Aadhaar-based eKYC requires AUA/KUA licensing.

## Open Networks

- [ONDC](https://ondc.org) - Open Network for Digital Commerce — protocol-based, interoperable e-commerce.

## Communication (SMS, Email, Voice, WhatsApp)

- [MSG91](https://msg91.com) - SMS, email, voice, and WhatsApp APIs with OTP flows.
- [Gupshup](https://www.gupshup.io) - Conversational messaging across WhatsApp, SMS, and RCS.
- [Exotel](https://exotel.com) - Cloud telephony, voice, and SMS APIs.
- [Plivo](https://www.plivo.com) - Voice and messaging APIs (India-founded, global coverage).

## AI & LLMs Built in India

- [Sarvam AI](https://www.sarvam.ai) - Models and APIs focused on Indian languages.
- [Krutrim](https://www.olakrutrim.com) - AI cloud and models for Indic use cases.

## Maps & Location

- [Mappls (MapmyIndia)](https://www.mappls.com) - Maps, geocoding, and routing APIs with strong India coverage.

## Developer Tools Made in India

- [Hasura](https://hasura.io) - Instant GraphQL APIs over Postgres and other data sources.
- [Appsmith](https://www.appsmith.com) - Open-source low-code internal-tool builder.
- [ToolJet](https://www.tooljet.com) - Open-source low-code platform for internal tools.
- [Hoppscotch](https://hoppscotch.io) - Open-source, lightweight API request builder.
- [Frappe / ERPNext](https://frappe.io) - Open-source web framework and ERP.
